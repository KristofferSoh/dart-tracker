    📋 Tournament rules

    1. Three throws per day.
    2. You may throw one day forward or one day back — but at most two days' worth in a single day (today + tomorrow, or today + yesterday).
    3. If you throw for two days in one day, another player has to throw in between the two rounds.
    4. You can't throw for Monday on Friday, or for Friday on Monday.

    The app flags a save that looks like it breaks these, but never blocks it — whether someone threw between two rounds is on you to sort out.
